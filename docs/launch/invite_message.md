## Version B - for people who don't trade but you want feedback from

```
Hey - soft-launching a small thing.

It's a weekly signal for gold. One direction call per week. Not a
subscription, just a Telegram channel that posts every Sunday night.

I'm inviting you because I want honest feedback on whether the messages
make sense to someone who isn't already in the trading world. Not
whether you'd trade it - whether you can follow what it's saying.

Channel: [Telegram channel link]

If you have 30 seconds one Sunday to read a post and tell me if it made
sense, I'd owe you one. Otherwise ignore.
```

## Notes

- Do not send to more than ~10 people in the first invite. The point is
  early feedback, not scale. Public promotion can come later after we've
  seen a few directional calls resolve.
- Version A goes to people who will actually consider trading it. They
  need enough info to decide.
- Version B goes to people whose feedback on the messaging you value.
  They shouldn't feel pressured to trade.
- Do not use either version on Twitter, LinkedIn, or any broadcast
  medium. This is one-to-one for the soft launch.
