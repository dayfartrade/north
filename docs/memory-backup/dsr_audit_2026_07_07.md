**Date:** 2026-07-07 pm, after reading Ch 14 of *Advances in Financial ML*.

**Setup:** v7.2.1 shipped with permutation p=0.032 on the 60/40 walk-forward.
Janus flagged the multi-hypothesis under-correction that afternoon. Reading
Ch 14.7.3 gave the finance-specific formula (Deflated Sharpe Ratio) that
turns Janus's concern into a hard number.

## Numbers

| Metric | Value |
|---|---|
| n trades | 52 |
| Mean/trade | +$812 |
| Per-trade Sharpe (raw) | 0.4479 |
| Annualized Sharpe (at 240 trades/yr scaling) | 6.94 |
| Skewness | -0.710 |
| Kurtosis (non-excess) | 2.81 |
| **PSR vs SR=0** | **0.9965** ✓ |
| **DSR with N=3 (shipped variants only)** | **0.1753** ✗ |
| **DSR with N=15 (all trials, honest count)** | **0.0000** ✗ |

Assumption: V[SR_n] = 0.5 (LdP's illustrative default, since I did not
record per-variant SRs at test time). Real value could shift DSR up or down.

## Interpretation

- **PSR clears easily** — the observed edge is likely non-zero vs pure noise.
- **DSR fails at both trial counts** — after correcting for how many variants
  I tested, the observed Sharpe is below the expected maximum under H0.

## Why this doesn't trigger a revert

- v7.2.1 passed all its pre-registered gates AT THE TIME of shipping.
- Phase-7 gates + walk-forward + permutation were the discipline we had.
- DSR discipline is being installed NOW, going forward.
- Reverting a live strategy on a post-hoc discipline change would itself be
  a discretionary act contaminated by the same selection bias.

## What it DOES change

1. **Live data is the only trustable evidence going forward.** Backtest results
   are contaminated by selection bias. Ship gate on live-only from here.
2. **DSR is a hard gate for every future strategy-engine or session-config
   change:** DSR > 0.95 against the honest N of tested variants that month.
3. **Every registered experiment must record per-trade SR at test time** so
   V[SR_n] is measured, not assumed.
4. **Marcos's Third Law adopted:** "Every backtest result must be reported in
   conjunction with all the trials involved in its production." Every future
   ship commit message will list N and DSR.

## Retroactive honesty

The "walk-forward p=0.032 → signal is real" claim I posted earlier today was
under-corrected. Under N=15 Bonferroni it needed p<0.003 (per Janus). Under
DSR with N=15 it needed >0.95. Both fail.

The shipped strategy MAY have real edge — but the backtest evidence is not
strong enough to prove it. The next 20-100 live trades will settle it.
