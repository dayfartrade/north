Governance principles (unchanged from day 1)

  • Pre-registration. Every candidate's signal + parameters + gates frozen publicly BEFORE any backtest results are seen.
  • Append-only. Once published, calls are never edited or deleted. Winners and losers stay in the record.
  • Public retirement. If a shipped product diverges from backtest, it retires publicly with full disclosed track record.
  • OOS discipline. Sample splits decided before testing. Hold-out windows never touched during design.
  • Honest about failure. More retirements than shipments. Every rejection documented.
  • No hidden signals. No paywall on any trading call, ever. Monetization is community + analysis only.
