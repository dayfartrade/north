How to read this

  • shipped — passed ship gates, running as public product.
  • shadow_beta — shipped with disclosed beta status; live-tracking is the final validation.
  • shadow / pre_registered_shadow — logged in parallel to a shipped product, not published publicly, awaiting forward validation.
  • pre_registered — signal + gates locked in; backtest may or may not have completed.
  • rejected / rejected_ship_gates / rejected_oos_test — failed one or more pre-committed thresholds. Public retirement.
  • deprecated_by_dependency — retired because a parent trial was rejected.

Why publish rejections?

If we hide the failures, we shouldn't be trusted with the winners. Publication bias is the biggest single source of overfitting in quantitative research. Every rejection here counts toward the Bonferroni-adjusted significance thresholds for anything we do ship.
