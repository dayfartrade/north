How it works

  • Session ORB. First 30 minutes of each major session establishes the opening range.
  • Breakout with trend filter. Trade in the direction of the 1h EMA-50 slope, if the range breaks.
  • Fixed geometry per session. London uses a fixed stop; New York and Asia use OR-range stops with a target multiple.
  • 5-box audit gate. Signal · stand-down (news/fix) · funding regime · basis divergence · CFTC COT context. All must pass.
  • Time-boxed. Cancel if no breakout in 60 min. Time-exit at 2 hours max.
