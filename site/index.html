How we operate

  • Pre-registration. Every candidate's signal + parameters + gates are frozen publicly BEFORE any backtest results are seen.
  • Append-only track records. Once published, calls are never edited or deleted. Winners and losers stay.
  • Public retirement. If a shipped product's live results diverge from backtest, it retires publicly with full disclosed track record.
  • OOS discipline. Sample splits are decided before testing. Hold-out windows are never touched during design.
  • Honest about failure. We've retired more strategies than we've shipped. Every rejection is documented.

What we retired

Because if we hide the failures, we shouldn't be trusted with the winners.

  • Session ORB gold day-trading (v7 family). Shipped 2026-07-01, halted 2026-07-13 after SPRT crossed halt boundary at n=18. 12-year OOS confirmed the ORB mechanism family is broken on modern gold intraday.
  • Path Z (NY-SHORT + Low-ER + Mon-Wed). Discovered 2026-07-20 from 2024-2026 gold data. Rejected 2026-07-22 after 9-year pre-2024 OOS showed WR 44% and mean +$2.58/trade — a 2025-2026 regime artifact, not a persistent edge.
  • Fast bot scalping on 1m gold. Two mechanisms tested (post-event momentum, session-transition), both fell below pre-committed retire thresholds.
  • Gap-fade, Meyers HR-ORB, Crabel patterns, event conditioning, cross-market pooling. All tested on 12-year data. All rejected.
