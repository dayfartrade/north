How the call is made

  • 4-week momentum. Gold close vs 20 trading days ago. Positive → LONG-eligible; Negative → SHORT-eligible.
  • 12-week momentum. Same, 60 trading days. Both must agree with 4-week.
  • Moving-average filter. 10-day SMA vs 40-day SMA. Trend must match direction.
  • Real-yield conditioning. 10y TIPS yield 4-week change: falling supports LONG; rising supports SHORT.
  • All four must agree. Otherwise the call is FLAT (no position). Historically ~55% of weeks are FLAT.
  • Position management. Entry Monday NY open. Stop = 2× ATR(20d). Exit = Friday 21:00 UTC close or stop hit, whichever first.
  • Instrument. Any liquid gold: GC futures, MGC micro-futures, GLD ETF, IAU ETF.

Pre-registered discipline

The signal + parameters were fixed in a public pre-reg before any backtest results were seen.

  • Signal frozen 2026-07-22 11:00 UTC. No post-hoc tuning of momentum periods, MA windows, ATR multiplier, or macro filter.
  • Sample split fixed pre-backtest. Training 2015-2020, OOS 2021-2023, Hold-out 2024-2026 — decided before any test.
  • Beta status. Training-window Sharpe (0.478) narrowly missed the 0.5 ship gate. Extended 16-year sample (added later as supplementary OOS) clears the 2 other borderline gates: bootstrap 95% CI on mean weekly return is [+0.013%, +0.447%] (clears zero), PSR vs SR=0 is 0.9785 (above 0.95).
  • Retirement criteria. If 12 months of live results diverge from backtest (weekly return > 0, WR ≥ 50%), the product retires publicly with full track record disclosed.

Risk profile — read this before trading it

  • Single-trade losses can be substantial. 2×ATR stops mean position risk scales with gold's volatility. In February–March 2026 (~$5,000 gold), two LONG stops hit for -$34k each on 100-oz contracts.
  • Losing years happen. 4 of 17 backtest years were losing. Worst year: 2017 (-$16k per contract).
  • Drawdowns lag recoveries. Second-worst drawdown lasted 86 weeks (2016–2019) before new equity high. Live subscribers must be able to hold through year-plus drawdowns.
  • Position sizing is critical. A useful rule: max 1–2% of account risked per call. At 2×ATR = $200 (typical), one 100-oz contract risks ~$20k. So $1M+ account for 1-contract trades, or use MGC micro (10-oz, $2k risk) for smaller accounts.
  • Backtest is not a guarantee. The signal survived 16 years but 2027+ could look nothing like the past.
