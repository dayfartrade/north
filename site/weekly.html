How the call is made

  • 4-week momentum. Gold close vs 20 trading days ago. Positive → LONG-eligible; Negative → SHORT-eligible.
  • 12-week momentum. Same, 60 trading days. Both must agree with 4-week.
  • Moving-average filter. 10-day SMA vs 40-day SMA. Trend must match direction.
  • Real-yield conditioning. 10y TIPS yield 4-week change: falling supports LONG; rising supports SHORT.
  • All four must agree. Otherwise the call is FLAT (no position).
  • Position management. Entry Monday NY open. Stop = 2× ATR(20d). Exit = Friday close or stop, whichever first.
